Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

Nagpur District Agro Dealers Association (NDADA) ("we", "us", or "our") operates the NDADA mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our App to register and manage your membership with the association.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, verify membership eligibility, and issue official certificates, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email address, phone number, physical address, and district.
  • Firm Information: Firm name, firm type, firm address, license numbers (general, pesticide, fertilizer, seed-cotton), GST number, IFMS number, and contact details.
  • Verification Documents: Uploaded files including photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar card, license copies) and applicant photos.
  • Identity Numbers: Aadhaar card number, which is strictly used for verification purposes and masked (XXXX-XXXX-1234) in all displays.
  • Payment Information: Details regarding membership fees paid, payment status, verification logs, and transaction reference IDs. Credit card, UPI, or banking details are handled directly by our payment processor (Razorpay) and are never stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Verify your identity and eligibility as an agro dealer.
  • Process your membership application and issue certificates.
  • Facilitate secure payments for membership fees.
  • Provide updates, notices, and communications regarding association affairs.
  • Perform internal administration and comply with legal requirements.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the App:

Supabase — We use Supabase for secure cloud authentication, database storage, file hosting, and backend serverless operations. Supabase processes data on secure, SOC2-compliant infrastructure.

Razorpay — We use Razorpay to process online membership payments securely. Your credit card, UPI, or banking details are handled directly by Razorpay in compliance with industry security regulations (PCI-DSS). We do not store your payment instrument details.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information as long as your membership is active or as required for administrative and legal purposes.

In accordance with Google Play Store policy, you have the right to request deletion of your account at any time via the Profile → Delete Account option within the App. Upon deletion:

  • Your personal identifying information (name, email, phone) will be anonymized.
  • Uploaded verification documents will be permanently removed from storage.
  • Payment references will be retained solely for financial auditing obligations.

You may also request account deletion by emailing us at the contact address below.

5. Data Security

We implement strict technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data, including:

  • Database Row Level Security (RLS) policies to ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Masking of sensitive fields like Aadhaar numbers in all user-facing displays.
  • All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Secure token-based authentication via Supabase Auth.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for professional agro dealers and is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 18 has provided us with personal data, we will delete such information immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
