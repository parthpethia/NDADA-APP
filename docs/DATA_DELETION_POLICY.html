Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At NDADA, we respect your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your account and associated data from the NDADA mobile application.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your account and personal data using either of the methods below:

✅ Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the NDADA app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down to the "Danger Zone" section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm your decision in the dialog that appears.
  6. Your account will be immediately processed for deletion.

📧 Method 2: Email Request

If you are unable to access the app, you may request account deletion by sending an email to:

parth@ndada.in

Please include your registered email address and/or phone number so we can verify your identity. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following actions are taken:

Data Type Action Taken Status
Name, Email, Phone Replaced with anonymized placeholders Anonymized
Physical Address Replaced with anonymized placeholders Anonymized
Aadhaar Number Permanently removed Deleted
Firm Information & License Numbers Replaced with anonymized placeholders Anonymized
Uploaded Documents (Photos, ID Proofs) Permanently deleted from cloud storage Deleted
Applicant Photo Permanently deleted from cloud storage Deleted
Authentication Credentials Permanently deleted Deleted
Payment Transaction References Retained for financial audit compliance Retained

Payment transaction references are retained solely for financial auditing and legal compliance obligations, and cannot be used to personally identify you after anonymization.

Processing Timeline

  • In-App deletion: Processed immediately upon confirmation.
  • Email requests: Processed within 7 business days of identity verification.
